DECISION / TWO-KEY RULE

Confidence does not separate right from wrong

Wrong answers and correct answers both scored 0.90.

Fragile-first: eligible egg votes take priority.

Full ball closure: at least 2 agreeing model families with mean confidence ≥ 0.9, or explicit operator selection.

Otherwise: the shared egg / ball cap is 0.70.

Toy thresholds. The egg rule has a confidence condition (or unknown confidence). Backend agreement does not prove correctness or hardware safety.
