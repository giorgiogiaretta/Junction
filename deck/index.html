THE IDEA

See the object before closing

The head camera supplies context.
The object label selects grasp behaviour.

Proposed head-to-hand tracking: a Rokoko sensor on the operator’s head locates the hand relative to the camera.

Tracking remains unproven on real frames; camera intrinsics are assumed. Vision selects a closure limit, not a measured force.

SAFETY DESIGN / PROVISIONAL

Design around the dangerous mistake

0.70shared closure cap
egg + ball

Ball at full closure only when confirmed: operator selection, or agreement plus confidence checks.

Unknown object or failure: conservative fallback.

First real G1 run: planned at 10% closure. With staff present, after the mock gate.

Closure levels are tuned in simulation. Confidence thresholds are toy, hand-picked rules; this is not a validated hardware safety guarantee.
